隐私政策

更新日期 2026-10-23 · English version

适用范围

本政策适用于 UTOPIX, LLC 发布的 叶子阅读 iOS 阅读应用。基础阅读无需账号。App 默认不配置在线服务，也不自动上传书籍、阅读记录或诊断。LeafRead 网站及你自行连接的服务有各自的隐私政策。

本机阅读数据

你通过系统文件选择器或“在 叶子阅读 中打开”选择的 EPUB、PDF、TXT 和阅读包在设备上解析。正文、插图、阅读进度、书签、笔记、保存的译文与对话存储在应用的本机数据库中。PDF 原页副本保存在应用私有目录，并排除在设备备份之外。其他本机数据可能随你的 iOS 设备备份设置备份。PDF 文字识别在设备上完成；朗读使用系统语音能力。

你主动连接的在线服务

如果你在账号或设置页面输入 HTTPS 服务地址并连接，该服务会接收连接请求及通常的网络信息，例如 IP 地址。登录后，服务可向 App 提供姓名、邮箱、用户标识及会话信息；登录凭据保存在 iOS Keychain 中。只有在你主动发起在线 AI 操作时，必要的书籍信息、原文片段、问题和相关对话上下文才会发送给所选服务及其配置的模型提供方。手机书库不会因此自动同步到云端。请在连接前查看该服务及模型提供方的使用、保留和删除规则；你可以不连接而继续本地阅读。

可选的账号书库

如果所选服务支持账号书库，并且你单独授权书库同步后进入账号书库，该书库中的书籍正文和支持的插图、阅读进度、笔记与书签会与所选服务同步，并可能在后台切换回前台或本机内容变更后继续同步。账号书库与本机书库分开保存；只有你明确选择复制的本机书籍才会进入账号书库。保存的译文、AI 对话历史、阅读设置以及保留的 PDF 原文件不通过书库同步上传。返回本机书库会停止当前账号书库的自动同步，但不会删除服务端已有副本；删除服务端数据请使用对应服务的管理或删除功能。

诊断、分享与客服

App 的操作类别、耗时、结果等诊断保存在本机，不含书籍正文、笔记、搜索词或密钥，不会自动上传。导出阅读包、笔记或诊断会打开系统分享面板，只有你选定的接收方获得文件。联系客服时，你主动发送的邮箱、消息和附件将用于处理请求；不要发送密码或 API Key。客服信息保留至处理所需期间，之后可联系支持邮箱请求删除。

保留、删除和撤回

你可以在书籍菜单移除本机书籍及关联内容，删除单条笔记，或导出阅读包进行备份。退出已连接的账号会移除本机凭据并尝试撤销服务端会话；请在卸载前退出账号，Keychain 凭据可能不会随卸载清除。通过 iOS“删除 App”可移除应用沙盒中的本机数据；你自行保存或分享的备份需另行删除。服务端账号和模型请求记录的删除由对应服务处理，请通过其账号管理页面或支持渠道提出请求。

隐私承诺与联系

叶子阅读 iOS 不包含广告，不进行跨应用跟踪，不出售阅读数据，也不自动启用第三方分析或会话录屏。我们不要求通讯录、定位、相册或麦克风权限来阅读。政策更新会在此页面显示日期。隐私问题与删除请求请联系 leafread-support@googlegroups.com。

Privacy policy

Updated 2026-10-23

Scope

This policy covers the LeafRead iOS reader published by UTOPIX, LLC. Basic reading requires no account. No online service is configured by default, and the app does not automatically upload books, reading activity or diagnostics. The LeafRead website and services you choose to connect have their own privacy policies.

Reading data on your device

EPUB, PDF, TXT and reading packs you select through the system document picker or Open in LeafRead are parsed on your device. Text, illustrations, positions, bookmarks, notes, saved translations and conversations are stored in the app’s local database. Retained original PDFs are stored in the private app directory and excluded from device backups. Other local data may be backed up according to your iOS settings. PDF text recognition runs on-device; read aloud uses system speech capabilities.

Services you choose to connect

If you enter and connect an HTTPS service address in Account or Settings, that service receives connection requests and ordinary network information such as your IP address. After sign-in, it may provide the app with your name, email, user identifier and session information; authentication credentials are stored in iOS Keychain. Only when you initiate online AI actions are necessary book details, source passages, questions and relevant conversation context sent to the selected service and its configured model provider. Connecting does not automatically upload or synchronize your phone library. Review the service’s and model provider’s use, retention and deletion policies before connecting. You can continue local reading without connecting.

Optional account library

If your chosen service supports account libraries and you separately authorize library synchronization and open that library, its book text and supported illustrations, reading positions, notes and bookmarks are synchronized with that service. Synchronization may continue when you return to the foreground or edit library content. Account and device libraries are stored separately; device books enter the account library only when you explicitly copy them. Saved translations, AI conversation history, reading preferences and retained original PDF files are not uploaded through library synchronization. Returning to the device library stops automatic synchronization of the current account library without deleting existing server copies. Use the selected service’s management or deletion features to remove server data.

Diagnostics, sharing and support

Operation categories, timings and results stay on-device. Diagnostics exclude book text, notes, searches and keys and are never uploaded automatically. Exporting reading packs, notes or diagnostics opens the system share sheet; only recipients you choose receive the files. If you contact support, the email address, messages and attachments you send are used to handle your request. Do not send passwords or API keys. Support correspondence is retained as needed to resolve your request; contact support to request deletion.

Retention, deletion and withdrawal

Use a book’s menu to remove its local data and associated content, delete individual notes, or export a reading pack for backup. Signing out removes local credentials and attempts to revoke the server session. Sign out before uninstalling: Keychain credentials may survive app removal. Delete App in iOS removes the local app sandbox; separately delete backups you saved or shared. The service you connected controls deletion of its account and model request records; use that service’s account-management or support channel.

Privacy choices and contact

LeafRead iOS has no advertising, cross-app tracking, sale of reading data, automatic third-party analytics or session replay. Reading does not require Contacts, Location, Photos or Microphone permission. Policy updates are dated on this page. For privacy questions and deletion requests, contact leafread-support@googlegroups.com.
